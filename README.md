Problem Statement: Insufficient Parking Capacity Relative to Employee Headcount
Problem Statement:
The number of parking spaces on the office premises is insufficient for the size of the 
workforce. In addition, the current allocation model assigns spaces to specific individuals & 
Projects, which leads to underutilization. As a result, the shortage creates ongoing 
operational challenges.
Current State 
Supply: Total available parking slots = X [replace with actual number] 
Demand: Total associates with personal vehicles = Y [replace with actual number] 
Gap: Deficit of Y - X slots, resulting in a ratio of ~Z:1 associates per slot 
Peak Load: 85-95% of associates arrive between 8:30-10:00 AM, causing severe congestion 
Allocation Method: First-come-first-serve with no formal reservation or priority system & 
designated parking space for specific individuals.
Key Pain Points Observed 
Productivity Loss, Employee Dissatisfaction, Operational Disruption, Inequity Issues, 
Environmental & Cost Impact.
Stakeholders Impacted 
Associates, Facilities/Admin Team, Security Team, Brand positioning. 
Constraints & Considerations 
Lesser options for expanding parking spaces, continuous improvement in associate returning 
to office.
Objective of Solution 
To design and implement a fair, scalable, and cost-effective parking management system that 
optimizes existing capacity, reduces associate stress, minimizes productivity loss, and aligns 
with hybrid work patterns. 
App Name: Mi Lugar
Data required: Synthetic data for a company size of 1500 associates with 150 parking space.
(Employee #, Name, Vehicle details, contact number, parking required, Open for carpooling, 
preferred carpooling spot, Monthly learning points). 
Assumption - 25% of employees needs car parking space.
App Requirements: Must Have features.
1) Login requirements (all the above data to be collected from the registration details).
2) 2 different views – One for the user and one for the admin to update the daily 
parking available spaces.
3) 100 parking spaces for designated associates from projects. 50 parking spaces based 
on the top 50 learning points improved associate.
4) Once employee’s login, app should show the available and utilized parking slots with 
employee details. Should show any unauthorized parking.
5) All parking spaces to be opened to all associates after 11 AM.
6) Once Parked, associates need to update in the app that they are parked. Vice versa 
when they leave.
7) Auto generated pass for Priority parking (top learners) 
Good to have Features (Upcoming):
1) Carpooling Feature.
2) Auto updated parking slots availability based on visual intelligence.
3) GPS Enabled feature indicating if the parking slots are available when the car is in the 
vicinity of the office.
